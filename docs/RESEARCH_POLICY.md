# Fresh evidence policy

User requirement: next steps must be informed by newly studied research papers and current online best practices, not solely by pre-downloaded skills.

Before every material development checkpoint, run a focused live search and inspect primary sources relevant to its decision. Recheck at the next checkpoint, on dependency changes, contradictory evidence or a changed API. Record checked date/time and version; distinguish publication date from retrieval date.
Read at least one relevant paper's applicable section and official documentation for the touched technology. If retrieval is unavailable, record the gap and do not present memory or snippets as a fresh study. Proceed only on already verified evidence for low-risk work; do not close a research-dependent checkpoint as satisfied until its missing evidence is resolved.
Newly studied means inspected now; it need not mean published today. Do not add unrelated papers to satisfy a quota.

## Evidence record
Question; search query; source title/URL; publication/version and checked date; inspected section/depth; concise finding; limitation; proposed decision; accept/reject/defer; affected requirement/test; next recheck.
Use original arXiv/publisher/author papers and official framework/database/testing documentation. Blogs and skill instructions can suggest a question, but cannot alone settle a material design choice.
Preprints are not automatically peer-reviewed. Never infer acceptance from a template conference heading. Do not transfer benchmark percentages into promised ReturnDesk performance.
A source claim and our engineering adaptation must be visibly distinct. New research never overrides assignment stack, business rules or the frozen scope without an explicit decision record.
Skills remain useful procedural aids; compare their advice with fresh evidence and the repository contracts.

## Budget and stop rule
Use a focused research pass proportionate to the decision and deadline. Stop when evidence resolves the choice; record no relevant new evidence honestly. Reuse inspected sources within a coherent checkpoint rather than researching every keystroke.
